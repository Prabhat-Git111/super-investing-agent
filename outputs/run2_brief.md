# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — Research Brief

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a cable manufacturing company that produces power and data cables, reporting Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore (or ₹1,428 crore depending on the source) and a net profit of ₹82 crore [S1, 2026-08-09; S2, 2026-08-11; S3, 2026-08-08]. The company is expanding capacity via a new plant in Bharuch and maintains a growing order book alongside rising export contributions [S2, 2026-08-11; S3, 2026-08-08].

## Bull case
- **Order Book Growth:** The order book expanded to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat—which represents a total capex of ₹620 crore funded primarily through internal accruals and some term debt—is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will add about 30% to cable capacity [S2, 2026-08-11; S3, 2026-08-08]. Management expects Bharuch to contribute starting in the second half of the year [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Data-Centre & Export Momentum:** Data-centre cables comprise roughly 9% of domestic revenue and have the potential to double within two years [S2, 2026-08-11]. Additionally, exports rose to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Guidance & Leverage:** Management maintained its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance at 15 to 17 percent [S2, 2026-08-11], supported by a conservative net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35 times [S2, 2026-08-11].

## Bear case
- **Margin Pressures:** Operating and EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 (compared to 12.9% in Q1 FY26) driven by a nearly 14% increase in copper costs [S1, 2026-08-09; S3, 2026-08-08]. 
- **Working Capital Strain:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the past year due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Regulatory and Tax Demands:** The company received an order from CGST Mumbai demanding ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) regarding input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S7, 2026-09-02].

## Open questions
- **Revenue Figure Discrepancy:** Business Daily Online reported Q1 revenue at ₹1,428 crore (a 35% YoY increase) [S1, 2026-08-09], whereas Sarvottam Cables Ltd's official investor relations press release stated Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was ₹1,248 crore (an 18.0% YoY increase) [S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Promoter Pledging Discrepancy:** Market Watch India reported in March 2024 that promoter pledging stood at 35% [S4, 2024-03-18], while official NSE filings for June 2026 state that pledged promoter shares represent only 4.1% of promoter holdings [S8, 2026-07-15].
- **Entity Confusion:** Clarification is needed to separate Sarvottam Cables Ltd (the power/data cable manufacturer) from unrelated local operators such as "Sarvottam Cable Network" in Nagpur, which faced municipal penalties for unauthorized overhead wiring [S6, 2026-08-20].
- **Monitoring Items:** How quickly raw material cost increases can be fully passed through under contracts with 1-to-2 quarter lag price-variation clauses, and how the CGST tax demand will be resolved.
