# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — Research Brief

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures and supplies cables with a growing order book of ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 [S3, 2026-08-08]. For Q1 FY27, the company reported an official revenue of ₹1,248 crore and a profit after tax of ₹82 crore [S3, 2026-08-08]. The company is expanding capacity by 30% with its new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant scheduled to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3, 2026-08-08].

## Bull case
- **Order Book Expansion:** Total order book grew to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore in the previous year [S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Capacity Growth:** The new Bharuch plant increases cable capacity by approximately 30% and is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27, with contributions expected in the second half of the year [S2, 2026-08-11, S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Export Momentum:** Exports increased to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Niche Demand:** Data-centre cables comprise roughly 9% of domestic revenue and are projected by management to double within two years [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Improving Promoter Pledge:** Official shareholding patterns show that pledged promoter shares declined to 4.1% in June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [S8, 2026-07-15].

## Bear case
- **Margin Pressure:** Operating and EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 due to an approximate 14% rise in copper prices [S1, 2026-08-09, S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Working Capital Strain:** Receivable days extended from 78 to 96 over the past year due to slower payments from two major state utility customers [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Regulatory and Tax Liabilities:** The company received a ₹46.3 crore demand order (inclusive of interest and penalty) from the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, regarding input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S7, 2026-09-02].
- **Debt and Capex Obligations:** Total Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore (70% spent), funded via internal accruals and term debt, resulting in a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35 times [S2, 2026-08-11].

## Open questions
- **Revenue Discrepancy:** Business Daily Online reported Q1 FY27 revenue at ₹1,428 crore (+35%) based on an exchange filing [S1, 2026-08-09], whereas the official company press release reports revenue at ₹1,248 crore (+18%) [S3, 2026-08-08]. Which figure is correct?
- **Promoter Pledging Conflict:** Market Watch India reported promoter pledging at 35% in March 2024 [S4, 2026-03-18], while official NSE shareholding filings report promoter pledging at 11.5% for June 2025 and 4.1% for June 2026 [S8, 2026-07-15]. 
- **Entity Confusion:** Does the ₹8 lakh municipal penalty for unauthorised overhead cables apply to Sarvottam Cables Ltd or an unrelated local operator named "Sarvottam Cable Network"? [S6, 2026-08-20]
- **Tax Order Impact:** How will management contest or provision for the ₹46.3 crore CGST demand, and what will be its cash flow impact? [S7, 2026-09-02]
