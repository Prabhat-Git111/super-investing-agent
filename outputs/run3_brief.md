# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — Research Brief

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors, reporting Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore (up 18.0% YoY) and a PAT of ₹82 crore [S3, 2026-08-08]. The company maintains a full-year revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent [S2, 2026-08-11] backed by a growing order book of ₹3,900 crore [S3, 2026-08-08].

## Bull case
- **Robust Order Book & Export Growth:** The order book expanded to ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, compared to ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S3, 2026-08-08]. Export contributions grew to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3, 2026-08-08].
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity and represents a total capex of ₹620 crore (70% already spent), is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Emerging Growth Segments:** Data-centre cables currently comprise about 9 percent of domestic revenue and management projects this segment to double within two years [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Improved Governance/Pledging Trends:** Official shareholding filings indicate that promoter pledged shares have decreased to 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [S8, 2026-07-15].

## Bear case
- **Margin Pressure:** Q1 EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points YoY to 11.2% due to rising costs in primary raw materials like copper, which rose nearly 14% [S1, 2026-08-09, S3, 2026-08-08]. 
- **Working Capital Risks:** Receivable days lengthened from 78 to 96 over the past year, primarily driven by delayed payments from two large state utility customers [S2, 2026-08-11].
- **Regulatory and Legal Liabilities:** CGST Mumbai issued a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (inclusive of interest and penalties) against the company concerning input tax credit claims for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S7, 2026-09-02].

## Open questions
- **Conflicting Revenue Reporting:** Business Daily Online (S1) reported Q1 revenue at ₹1,428 crore (35% growth) [S1, 2026-08-09], whereas the company's investor relations filing (S3) states Q1 revenue from operations was ₹1,248 crore (18% growth) [S3, 2026-08-08]. 
- **Entity Distinction:** Clarification is required regarding whether the Nagpur Municipal Corporation's penalty of ₹8 lakh for unauthorized overhead wiring applies to Sarvottam Cables Ltd or an unrelated local cable TV/broadband entity named Sarvottam Cable Network [S6, 2026-08-20].
- **Raw Material Lag Impact:** With roughly two-thirds of contracts utilizing price-variation clauses that reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [S2, 2026-08-11], how long will margin compression persist if copper and input costs remain volatile?
